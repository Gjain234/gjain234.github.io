I’ve started realizing that a close group of friends is a gift that keeps on giving. Here are a few fun games I created to discover new layers in my friendships.

---

## <i class="fa-solid fa-link fa-xs" aria-hidden="true"></i> [Who Even Are You?](https://whoevenareyou-86aee.web.app/) 

This game is the perfect way to learn the randomest details about your friends that have never come up normally in conversation. Everyone writes anonymous slips about themselves; then the group argues, votes, and tries to guess who wrote what. The writer tries to throw others off. You'll be surprised what you find out.

---

## <i class="fa-solid fa-link fa-xs" aria-hidden="true"></i> [Common Thread](https://juqx6q2erfd6hfzelunaun.streamlit.app/) 

This one generates random subsets of people in the group. Your job: be the first to name something that is true of everyone in that subset, and *only* them. The first person to call out a trait that fits just those people wins that round. Fast, silly, and great for finding the weird overlaps in your group.
